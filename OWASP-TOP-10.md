# What is OWASP?

**OWASP** stands for **Open Worldwide Application Security Project**. It's a non profit foundation that works to improve software security. Think of it as a free, open community that produces tools, documentation, and standards than anyone can use. The most famous thing OWASP produces is the OWASP Top 10 — a regularly updated list of the 10 most critical web application security risks. It's essentially the industry's "most wanted" list of vulnerabilities.

## A01:2025 — Broken Access Control
Think of a web app like a building. Authentication is the security guard at the door checking your ID. Access Control is the system of locked doors inside the building — only certain people can enter certain rooms.  
**Broken Access Control** means those internal locks are either missing, broken, or bypassable. A regular user can walk into the admin server room. Access control enforces policy such that users cannot act outside of their intended permissions — failures lead to unauthorized disclosure, modification, or destruction of data, or performing business functions beyond a user's limits. owasp
